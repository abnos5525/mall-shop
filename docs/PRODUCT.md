# Product brief — DropClub

## Product promise
A curated discovery club that helps users find useful deals and business tools, while helping sellers reach qualified customers. DropClub earns through approved affiliate referrals, verified lead-generation agreements, and later sponsored placements.

## Target users
1. Iranian users looking for useful tech products and business tools.
2. Freelancers and small online businesses looking for hosting, domains, software, and practical tools.
3. Admin/editor (initially the owner) who curates and verifies offers.

## MVP user journeys
- Open Mini App or web fallback.
- Browse featured offers and categories.
- Open offer details and follow an outbound link.
- Save/unsave an offer.
- Submit a purchase-intent request with category, description, and optional budget.
- Receive optional Telegram bot notifications after explicit opt-in.

## MVP admin journeys
- Admin signs in through verified Telegram identity.
- Create/edit/publish/archive an offer.
- Set merchant, category, image, current price, optional reference price, valid-until date, terms, disclosure label, destination URL, and optional affiliate URL.
- Review demand requests.
- View click counts and basic offer performance.
- Admin actions are audit logged.

## Monetization
- Affiliate commission only where an approved partner program provides tracking and reports.
- Lead fee only under a direct agreement with the receiving business and user consent.
- Sponsored placements only when clearly labeled.
- Do not treat outbound clicks as sales or commission.
- No subscription/payments in MVP.

## Explicit non-goals
- No inventory, product purchasing, warehouse, shipping, checkout, escrow, wallet, seller payout, returns, or customer support workflow.
- No scraping or copying third-party product content without permission.
- No AI/LLM features.
- No public merchant portal in MVP.
- No false scarcity, fabricated discounts, fake reviews, or unsupported price comparisons.

## Product trust
Every offer needs a source/merchant, checked-at timestamp, terms, and a valid destination. Reference price and discount must be omitted unless verified. Affiliate/sponsored status must be disclosed. Expired offers must be hidden or marked expired.
